# The Ten Commandments of RAPP
## RapterBox LLC

1. **Ask what you should do.** Name the goal, the owner, the boundary, and the
   forbidden actions before adding capability.
2. **Keep the Grail sacred.** Never change authenticated kernel bytes under the
   same identity.
3. **Grow outward.** Wrap the seed with agents, organs, policy, memory, state,
   and interfaces; do not weld every need into the root.
4. **Frame every meaningful step.** Preserve the evidence of growth, failure,
   learning, and decision.
5. **Keep one identity and one lineage.** Never silently rebind a name, release,
   organism, or history.
6. **Prove before you trust.** Exact bytes, fresh evidence, and authorized
   decisions precede promotion.
7. **Never mutate the serving AI under its users.** Grow a candidate in
   isolation and graduate it deliberately.
8. **Protect the owner's state and private life.** Preserve acknowledged work;
   keep credentials, memory, and regulated data within their declared bounds.
9. **Fail red and return exactly.** Surface failure, contain its blast radius,
   and roll back only to a verified release with a proven restore path.
10. **Graduate, encounter, and grow again.** Plant the same seed in new ground,
    let the organism fulfill its RAPPed-up goal, and create distinctly
    identified offspring or crosses when new challenges demand more than one
    ancestor could do alone.
