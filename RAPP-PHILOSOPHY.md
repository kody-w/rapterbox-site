# Why RAPP
## RapterBox LLC foundational doctrine

Every RAPP begins with one question:

> **What should I do?**

You do not answer by welding every capability into one permanent program. You
RAPP up the problem.

Name the goal. Wrap it with one capability. Record what happened in a frame.
Test the result. Add, replace, or remove the next layer. Repeat until the thing
reliably does what was asked.

Layer after layer, frame after frame, a problem becomes a bounded digital
organism.

At the center is the **Grail**: the sacred, byte-identical kernel. RAPP grows
around it through protocol, identity, agents, organs, tools, policy, memory,
state, interfaces, deployment, and experience. The organism may become deeply
specialized without silently becoming a different species.

When the organism does what was asked, it **graduates**. It leaves the protected
nursery, enters the wild, and begins fulfilling its RAPPed-up goal for real
people. Graduation requires evidence, health limits, state continuity, bounded
autonomy, and an exact way home.

Because it is portable and self-documented, the organism can keep growing in
the wild. A new encounter becomes new frames and a new candidate. When that
candidate becomes materially different, it graduates as an **offspring** with
its own identity and an explicit link to the unchanged ancestor.

Different RAPPed organisms can also be **crossed**. Their documented bodies and
capabilities become parent material for a newly identified organism that may
solve a challenge neither could meet alone. Crossing never collapses the
parents, histories, rights, or authority into one. It creates a new descendant.

The lineage may keep trying without a final generation. Each attempt remains
bounded by policy, evidence, time, and resources; infinity belongs to the chain
of generations, not to an uncontrolled process.

## Why the name

RAPP is a name before it is an acronym:

- **R** is recursive. The organism grammar repeats from an agent to a
  rapplication, brainstem, neighborhood, and estate.
- **APP** is application. The result is runnable, useful life.
- Spoken aloud, RAPP carries **wrap**: capability wraps the Grail instead of
  cutting into it.

A RAPP is a recursive application organism. To RAPP is to wrap a problem in
verifiable layers and frames until the resulting organism can graduate to
fulfill that goal in the wild.

## The tree

The Grail is the seed. The RAPP/1 protocol is its membrane. Agents and organs
become branches. Memory and state become rings of lived continuity. Policy and
deployment become bark: protection at the boundary. Products are the visible
canopy.

The same seed can be planted in new ground:

- a person's machine;
- a business-controlled RapterBox;
- an enterprise tenant;
- a regional deployment cell;
- a provider-neutral future runtime.

The ground may differ. The root identity does not.

## The compact

> One open seed. Infinitely many owned organisms. No hidden mutation of the
> root.

The RAPP/1 specification and reference implementation are open so strangers can
implement the same trust substrate. Each organism may still own its identity,
memory, policy, data, authored experience, and commercial expression.

The canonical protocol philosophy is published at:
<https://github.com/kody-w/rapp-1/blob/main/PHILOSOPHY.md>.
