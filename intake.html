BUILT FOR OWNER-OPERATORS

Especially useful when the business lives in one person's head and one worn machine.

RBox Intake is designed for one-person businesses, consultants, and small operating teams that need to upgrade without starting their working life over.

  • You choose the source, scope, and workflows.
  • The source device stays read-only during intake.
  • Private records and credentials stay local.
  • Unknowns are stated instead of guessed.
  • No workflow is accepted without a test and rollback path.
