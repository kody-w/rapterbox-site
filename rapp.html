WHY RAPP

The organism grows around the Grail.

The Grail is the seed. RAPP is the living structure wrapped around it: protocol, identity, agents, organs, tools, policy, memory, state, interfaces, deployment, and experience.

The kernel is not a feature backlog. New capability grows outward as a removable, testable layer. This keeps the organism free to become specific without silently becoming a different species.

The name

R is recursive: the same organism grammar repeats from an agent to a rapplication, brainstem, neighborhood, and estate. APP is application: runnable, useful life. Spoken aloud, RAPP carries wrap: capability wraps the Grail instead of cutting into it.

RAPP is also a verb. To RAPP a problem is to wrap it in verifiable layers and frames until the resulting organism can be safely graduated.

1Name the goal.
State the owner, boundary, evidence, and forbidden actions.
2Add one layer.
Agent, organ, adapter, policy, state, interface, or deployment control.
3Frame the result.
Keep what happened inspectable and content-addressed.
4Try again.
Add, replace, or remove the next layer under bounded policy.
5Graduate.
When it reliably fulfills the goal, release it through evidence-bearing gates.
6Grow in the wild.
New encounters create new frames, candidates, and distinctly identified offspring.
CONSTITUTION I–VI

The law of the organism.

I · The Question Comes First

Every organism begins with “What should I do?” The goal and its boundaries are named before capability is added.

II · The Grail Is Sacred

The authenticated Grail remains byte-identical under its identity. Changed bytes are a different seed with explicit lineage.

III · Growth Moves Outward

New behavior belongs in agents, organs, adapters, policy, frames, configuration, state, controls, or skins.

IV · Growth Is Framed

Every material step leaves evidence. History is not rewritten to make the present look inevitable.

V · Identity Survives Change

An organism keeps one verifiable lineage across devices, releases, and environments. Names and locations do not silently rebind it.

VI · Ownership Is Local by Default

The owner controls memory, credentials, policy, and private records. Portable capability may travel; private lived history does not travel by accident.

One open seed. Infinitely many owned organisms. No hidden mutation of the root.
CONSTITUTION VII–XII

The law of graduation and life in the wild.

VII · Power Is Bounded

Autonomy expands through policy and evidence. Missing permission or degraded capability is never presented as success.

VIII · Graduation Is Earned

The exact candidate passes RAPP CI/CD, production-shaped Preprod, restore proof, and bounded RAPP Deploy exposure.

IX · Users Are Not the Test Environment

Serving behavior does not mutate in place. State survives promotion and rollback. Exposure grows by measured cells and cohorts.

X · The Seed Grows in New Ground

The same Grail can be planted on a new machine, tenant, region, or substrate. Environment is an outer layer.

XI · Red Is Truth

A red gate is protection, not obstruction. Repair the cause or prove the oracle wrong; never mute the finding.

XII · Innovation Must Remain Possible

The invariant core stays small. Checks, component kinds, objectives, controls, agents, and organs remain extensible.

Offspring and crossing

A self-documented organism may create a differently identified offspring after a new encounter. Two or more organisms may be crossed into a new descendant when their documented capabilities can solve a challenge none could meet alone. Parents remain immutable; identity, authority, ownership, and history never collapse.

The lineage may continue without a final generation. Every concrete attempt remains bounded by policy, evidence, time, and resources.

THE TEN COMMANDMENTS

Keep these when everything else changes.

1
Ask what you should do. Name the goal, owner, boundary, and forbidden actions.
2
Keep the Grail sacred. Never change authenticated kernel bytes under the same identity.
3
Grow outward. Wrap the seed; do not weld every need into the root.
4
Frame every meaningful step. Preserve evidence of growth, failure, learning, and decision.
5
Keep one identity and one lineage. Never silently rebind an organism or its history.
6
Prove before you trust. Exact bytes, fresh evidence, and authorized decisions precede promotion.
7
Never mutate the serving AI under its users. Grow a candidate in isolation and graduate it deliberately.
8
Protect the owner's state and private life. Preserve acknowledged work and declared data boundaries.
9
Fail red and return exactly. Contain the blast radius and roll back only to a verified release.
10
Graduate, encounter, and grow again. Fulfill the RAPPed-up goal; create attributable offspring or crosses when the wild demands more.
