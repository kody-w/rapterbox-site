HOLO ZOO / INTERNAL PREVIEW

Explore. Meet. Watch.

Holo Zoo is a local-first habitat for Rolling Core organisms. The current app combines an offline Holo Field, four local Houses, a commerce-free Companion mode, Holo playback, immutable history, and a non-executing work-interface walkthrough.

Current release boundary: adult internal TestFlight only. No real purchases, rentals, resale, managed-compute sales, production jobs, tips, sponsorship, public publication, Coin economics, GPS, or map provider.

Holo Field

A permissionless offline radar built from public organism IDs. No GPS or map provider. It requests no location and generates no physical destination.

Four Houses

Overwatch, Scout, Forge, and Sentinel organize perspective and community. House never changes power, price, encounters, companion capability, Growth, or economic weight.

Companion mode

Discovery, meeting, playback, history, and local relationship without commerce. Spending cannot buy affection, memory, survival, belonging, or the right to keep playing.

Work walkthrough

A visibly nonofficial UI walkthrough showing how bounded request, status, proof, and delivery screens may work later. It performs no job and creates no service or payment claim.

Local custody

Signed Rolling Core capsules can be inspected, stored, imported, and exported locally. External protocol interoperability is disabled in this build until the RAPP rev-14 migration is complete.

Constitution

The relationship and the game outrank the business model. Real commerce and irreversible publication remain fail-closed until their independent authority, recovery, safety, and shutdown gates pass.
