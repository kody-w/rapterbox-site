Who this is not for

We'd rather tell you now than after a call that never needed to happen.

  • Pre-production pilots. If your agents aren't shipping real decisions yet, we're premature — come back when they are.
  • Decisions that don't matter. If a wrong AI answer costs you nothing, you don't need this, and we won't pretend you do.
  • Anyone shopping for a dev tool. This is not an eval framework or a prompt tester. It's assurance for decisions that are already in the world.

If any of those is you today: keep the essay, it's yours. No hard feelings.

Who it is for

Teams with AI making consequential decisions in production — and someone (Risk, Audit, a security review, a regulator) who will eventually ask "prove that output was checked." If that sentence gave you a specific person's face: this page was written about your team.

What you get

The record. Your consequential AI decisions get independently verified before they ship — a wrong answer gets stopped in front of your human first — and every verdict leaves an audit-grade, tamper-evident record your auditors can actually hold. It runs on your infrastructure; your questions and data don't leave your walls.

How an engagement runs

  1. Paid pilot — 30 days, fixed fee.One workflow of yours. Deliverable: a verification report over your real decisions, including where your current setup would have shipped a wrong answer.
  2. Platform — annual license.Priced by decisions verified, not by seats — the value scales with what's at stake, not headcount.
  3. Assurance pack.Exportable evidence bundles mapped to your control framework, built for the audit conversation.

There are honest limits, and we state them before you sign anything — in the first real conversation, not after the pilot. Ask us and we'll tell you exactly where this doesn't help.

Proof we believe it

We run our own company on it. Our pipeline and our decisions produce the same kind of verifiable records we sell — continuously, across multiple machines. We are our own first customer with something to lose.
