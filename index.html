How it works

  1. You talk. It records.Say it however it comes out. The paper trail builds itself — decisions, rules, what you tried, what broke. You never file anything.
  2. It does the reversible work.Repairs, drafts, reports, checks. Everything it does leaves a receipt you can verify without trusting us.
  3. It wakes you with what is actually yours.One message. The two or three calls only a human can make, with the work leading up to them already finished.

What it will not do

Worth knowing before you buy, not after.

  • It never sends, signs, pays or deletes on your behalf. There is no code path for those. A task needing one comes back as a decision, with the safe part already done.
  • It refuses to act on partial evidence. If it cannot see everything, it stops and says so rather than guessing confidently — the reason scheduled jobs fail silently on macOS is that most tools do the opposite.
  • It keeps working when the network drops and reconciles later. That is the right trade for a box in your office, and it is not a strongly consistent system.
  • Agreement is evidence, not proof. Machines that share a fault can agree and still be wrong, so the checks are spread across different systems on purpose.
