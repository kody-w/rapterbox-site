What we tell you before you buy

Agreement is evidence, not proof. Machines that share a defect can agree and still be wrong. We spread the checks across different systems on purpose, and we report how independent they actually are instead of hiding it behind a score.

It grades, suggests and waits. It does not sign things, spend money, or decide for you. The decisions stay yours — that is the design, not a limitation we plan to remove.

It keeps working when the network drops, and reconciles when it comes back. That is the right trade for a box in your office. It is not a strongly consistent system.
