# RapterBox Official Organism Market
## First Hatch Promise, resale, and standing bids

**Status:** Public prelaunch policy draft  
**Effective:** Only after rights clearance and commercial launch  
**Operator:** RapterBox LLC  
**Catalog:** Genesis 251 First Edition / First Dimension Originals

## 1. Fixed Original supply

There are exactly 251 First Edition Original catalog positions. An Original is
not duplicated to create resale inventory. Market activity transfers current
title among holders and RapterBox inventory.

Separately issued offspring are distinct organisms with distinct RAPPIDs,
histories, rights, and supply. They are never represented as another copy of a
First Edition Original.

## 2. Rip and hatch

A qualifying primary purchase begins with a **rip**: one rights-cleared,
available organism is selected from the disclosed eligible pool and delivered
as a signed title event plus its transferable Capsule.

Before launch, RapterBox will publish the inventory snapshot, selection method,
and verification evidence needed to audit that the rip came from the declared
pool. A hidden or changeable pool is not a valid rip.

The delivery frame starts the seven-day First Hatch period.

## 3. Seven-day First Hatch Promise

For seven calendar days after signed delivery:

- the buyer may hatch and evaluate the organism;
- the title is transfer-locked and cannot be listed or resold;
- the buyer may return it through the official return transaction for any
  reason;
- RapterBox takes current title and the transferable Capsule back into company
  inventory;
- RapterBox refunds the purchase price through the original payment path.

Taxes, processor fees, currency conversion, and network charges are refunded
to the extent recoverable or required by law. Fraud, duplicate refunds,
sanctions, chargeback abuse, and legally prohibited transactions remain
refusable.

The return never requires private memory, credentials, customer records,
private prompts, or local secrets. Those are not market inventory. Transferable
public lineage and rights-cleared mutations travel only when the applicable
title terms say they do.

Independent offspring created during the trial remain separate identities and
are not silently seized with the parent return.

## 4. Day seven: market eligibility

If the organism is not returned before the seven-day deadline:

1. the First Hatch Promise closes;
2. current title settles to the holder;
3. the organism becomes eligible for the official RapterBox market.

Market eligibility does not force a sale. The holder may keep the organism
unlisted, publish an asking price, accept an eligible bid, or withdraw a
listing under the market rules.

The seven-day transfer lock applies before the first resale. Later official
transfers may use their own settlement and acceptance window as disclosed at
the time of sale.

## 5. Official resale

The official market transfers current title, the declared transferable
Capsule, public lineage, and stated rights atomically. A listing must identify:

- the exact organism RAPPID;
- Original or offspring status;
- current title holder;
- included transferable artifacts and rights;
- excluded private state;
- asking price and expiry;
- current DOGG and provenance status.

RapterBox may charge disclosed market, custody, verification, payment, or
dealer fees. No fee may be hidden inside the title record.

## 6. RapterBox standing bids and buyouts

RapterBox may publish time-limited standing bids or direct buyout offers for
eligible organisms. Every offer states:

- eligible organism or class;
- offered price;
- quantity or budget limit;
- expiry;
- required provenance and condition;
- settlement terms.

An accepted offer transfers current title to RapterBox inventory. While a
funded bid is active, it is an observable market floor for the organisms it
actually covers. It is not a permanent guarantee.

RapterBox is simultaneously the original issuer, official market operator, and
potential bidder. That conflict is disclosed on every company bid.

## 7. No investment promise

Originals and offspring are digital organisms and collectible title records.
They are not RapterBox equity, do not pay dividends, and carry no promise of
profit, appreciation, liquidity, or a future buyout.

Historical sales, asking prices, or expired RapterBox bids are not guaranteed
floors. A floor exists only where an active funded bid can presently be
accepted.

Because random allocation and secondary markets may be regulated differently
by jurisdiction, commercial launch remains subject to rights clearance,
payments review, age and location controls where required, and qualified legal
review.

## 8. Evidence and amendments

Inventory snapshots, rips, deliveries, returns, settlements, listings,
transfers, and company bids are recorded as signed, content-addressed events.
This policy's Git history is its amendment history.

RapterBox may strengthen buyer protections or market integrity. It may not
retroactively duplicate an Original, rewrite a completed title event, absorb
private memory into a return, or describe a discretionary bid as guaranteed
value.
