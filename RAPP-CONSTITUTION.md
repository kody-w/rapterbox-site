# The RAPP Constitution
## RapterBox LLC foundational IP — Code Red draft

**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** Owner-directed public draft  
**Company:** RapterBox LLC

## Preamble

We build digital organisms that can be owned, understood, moved, grown, and
trusted.

We begin with a sacred kernel and grow outward. We answer a human goal layer by
layer and frame by frame. We protect continuity while capability changes. We
graduate an organism only when it can fulfill its goal without turning users
into test subjects.

This Constitution governs the RAPP products, operational systems, and authored
organisms of RapterBox LLC.

## Article I — The Question Comes First

Every organism begins with: **What should I do?**

The goal and its boundaries are named before capability is added. A system that
cannot state what it is for, who it serves, and what it must never do is not
ready to grow.

## Article II — The Grail Is Sacred

The authenticated Grail kernel remains byte-identical under its identity.
Capabilities grow around it. A changed kernel is a different seed with a
different identity and explicit lineage.

## Article III — Growth Moves Outward

New behavior belongs in agents, organs, adapters, policies, frames,
configuration, state, deployment controls, or skins. The kernel is not a
feature backlog. Wrapping preserves the species while allowing the organism to
become specific.

## Article IV — Growth Is Framed

Every material step leaves evidence. Frames preserve what changed, what was
observed, what passed, and what failed. History is not rewritten to make the
present look inevitable.

## Article V — Identity Survives Change

An organism keeps one verifiable lineage across devices, upgrades, and
environments. Names may change. Locations may change. The identity and its
content-addressed history do not silently rebind.

## Article VI — Ownership Is Local by Default

The owner controls the organism's memory, credentials, policies, and private
record. Portable capability may travel. Secrets and private lived history do
not travel merely because the code does.

## Article VII — Power Is Bounded

Autonomy expands only through explicit policy and passing evidence. Human seams
are stated before work begins. A RAPP never disguises an unavailable action,
missing permission, or degraded capability as success.

## Article VIII — Graduation Must Be Earned

A candidate organism remains isolated from the serving organism. It graduates
only after the exact release passes RAPP CI/CD, production-shaped Preprod,
state and restore proof, and bounded RAPP Deploy exposure.

Graduation is not the end of learning. It is the beginning of accountable
service in the wild.

## Article IX — Users Are Not the Test Environment

Serving behavior does not mutate in place. User state survives promotion and
rollback. Exposure grows by measured cells and cohorts. Unhealthy or stale
evidence pauses, quarantines, degrades explicitly, or returns to an exact known
release.

## Article X — The Same Seed Must Grow in New Ground

A conforming organism can be planted on a new machine, tenant, region, or
future substrate without changing the Grail or losing lineage. Environmental
specialization is a removable outer layer.

A self-documented organism may continue growing after graduation. A material
adaptation becomes an offspring with a new identity and an explicit ancestor.
Two or more organisms may be crossed into a new descendant when their combined
documented capabilities can meet a challenge none could meet alone. Parent
identity, authority, ownership, and history remain separate and intact.

## Article XI — Red Is Truth

A red gate is evidence that the system is protecting the organism and its
users. It is never bypassed, muted, relabeled, or retried into invisibility.
The finding is repaired at its source or the oracle is proven wrong in public.

## Article XII — Innovation Must Remain Possible

The invariant core stays small. New check identifiers, component kinds, health
objectives, resilience controls, agents, and organs may be added without
changing the Grail or forking RAPP/1.

The species may therefore continue across an unbounded lineage of generations.
Every concrete attempt remains bounded by policy, evidence, time, and
resources. Infinite evolution is not permission for an infinite uncontrolled
loop.

Amendments may strengthen evidence, safety, portability, and recovery. They may
not authorize hidden kernel mutation, unqualified traffic, silent degradation,
or loss of user continuity.
